How do I use it?

On the Draft tab, tap a slot and type or tap heroes. The picker fills each slot and jumps to the next empty one. Enter the enemy heroes (and your teammates if you know them). The best last picks update instantly. Choose your position and rank to narrow the list, then tap a suggestion to see exactly why it scores well, and Lock in.

What do the numbers mean?

Everything is in win-rate percentage points from recent ranked matches. A hero's draft score = its overall win-rate edge + its matchup advantage against each enemy + 0.6 × its synergy with each ally + a small bonus for filling a role your team lacks. In the app: More → How the scores work.

What is "They might pick next"?

While the enemy still has picks left, the app estimates their likely next heroes from how popular each hero is in your bracket and how well it fits their draft against yours. "Ban suggestions" lists the heroes that would counter your current team hardest.

How fresh is the data?

Statistics are rebuilt every day from about two million ranked public matches from the last three days (via OpenDota), split into three rank brackets. The app checks for new data when you open it and works offline with the last copy it has.

A new hero is missing

New heroes appear automatically once enough ranked games have been played with them, usually within a day or two of release.

Does it need an account?

No. There's no sign-up and no personal data. See the Privacy Policy.
