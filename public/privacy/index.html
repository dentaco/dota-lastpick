Privacy Policy

Last Pick for Dota 2 (iPhone app and dota-last-pick.vercel.app) · Updated 19 October 2026

Short version: Last Pick does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your draft, hero pool, hidden heroes and settings are saved only on your device (app storage on iPhone, browser storage on the web). They are never sent to us. Deleting the app or clearing your browser data removes them.

Network requests

  • Match statistics. The app downloads public hero statistics (data.json) from our website, hosted by Vercel. The request contains no personal information. Like any web server, Vercel may briefly keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and time) to run and protect the service; we do not use them to identify you.
  • Hero images. Hero portraits are bundled in the app. Only for heroes released after your app version, and on the website, images load from Valve's public Steam image server.
  • Website only: fonts load from Google Fonts.

The statistics themselves come from anonymous public match data published by OpenDota. They contain no information about you.

Children

Last Pick collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact
